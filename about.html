Who we are

TINKAS is a national management consulting firm specialized in Monitoring & Evaluation for the best consults in the field.

TINKAS Monitoring and Evaluation Consultants is a national management consulting firm registered under BRELA no. 466150, per the Business Registration Act (Cap 213) of Tanzania, with Tax Identification Number 116‑141‑108. The firm specializes in management consultancies for non-government organizations, faith-based organizations, and government institutions around Tanzania.

The company holds leading positions with its two business units in both Monitoring and Evaluation. Combining the experience of professionals from a variety of backgrounds, the firm delivers high-quality consulting and implementation support to address the needs of clients in Tanzania's public and private sectors.

Location & reach

Tinkas Monitoring and Evaluation Consults is based in Kagera Region, Bukoba Council District, Bilele ward, on Jamhuri Road near the Government Hospital — with easy access to clients that include faith-based organizations and non-government organizations, both non-profit and profit-making, from local and international institutions of different calibers.

The firm works majorly in health care, education, theology and religion, legal and human rights, nutrition and food technology, and economics. Our commitment is to provide brand-name advisory services of the highest quality, with the agility and flexibility that contemporary realities demand — delivering real benefits to all stakeholders through the work we do.

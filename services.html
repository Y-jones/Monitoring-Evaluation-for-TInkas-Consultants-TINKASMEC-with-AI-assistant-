M&E Service Lines

Our services

An experienced and skilled staff executing four categories of assignment — from systems design through to field delivery and final products.

M&E Training Consultancies

Systems & capacity

M&E systems development and assessment; routine and non-routine monitoring systems; data management processes; supportive supervision and data auditing; information utilization, research and evaluation; database development; theories, frameworks and tools development and application; digital and spatial M&E application; partnership and advocacy; capacity building.

M&E Field Consultancies

Evaluation & audit

Clarificatory, process and outcome evaluations of programs, projects and institutions for direct and indirect decision-making. Performance monitoring of client programs, projects and policies, reporting directly to funders, managers or officers. M&E compliance including data auditing and supportive supervision. Residential and commissioned research. Project appraisal assessment.

M&E Products Consultancies

Plans & instruments

M&E plan documents, Project Monitoring and Evaluation Plans (PMPs), project/program protocol indicators, data collection process tools, logical models and frameworks, organizational theories of change, data flow charts, processes and outcomes reporting tools, data quality assurance tools, digital data collection tools, assignment Terms of Reference (TOR), and project appraisal tools.

Other Consultancy Services

Strategy & comms

Strategic plan documents and strategic planning and management evaluation, organizational strategy development, organizational management manuals, Information Education Communication (IEC) materials development and dissemination, facilitation of organizational directive documents, and fundraising documents.

Data, Analytics & AI Services

Modern data capability

Data analytics, data science, machine learning, artificial intelligence, business intelligence and deep learning — audited, built and monitored with the same discipline as any M&E assignment. See the full Data & AI practice →

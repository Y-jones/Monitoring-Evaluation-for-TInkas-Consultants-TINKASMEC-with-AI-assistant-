About the firm

TINKAS Monitoring and Evaluation Consultants is registered under Tanzania's Business Registration Act (Cap 213), holding leading positions across two business units: Monitoring and Evaluation.

Based in Bukoba, Kagera Region — Bilele ward, Jamhuri Road, near the Government Hospital — the firm combines the experience of professionals from a variety of backgrounds to deliver consulting and implementation support to clients across Tanzania's public and private sectors.
